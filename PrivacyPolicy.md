# Privacy Policy & Data Handling Disclosure

## 1. Information We Collect and Process (VRC.Privacy.2)
Fishy Studios processes minimal user data strictly necessary to provide peer-to-peer multiplayer functionality. We collect and process:
* **Meta User ID & Basic Profile Data:** Authenticated via the Meta Horizon SDK to identify your account session.
* **Epic Online Services (EOS) Identifiers:** Product User IDs (PUIDs) and session tokens generated when connecting to peer-to-peer (P2P) multiplayer lobbies.

## 2. How Data is Used (VRC.Privacy.3)
We process the identifiers listed above solely for the following operational purposes:
* Authenticating players and establishing P2P multiplayer connections via Epic Online Services.
* Facilitating in-game matchmaking, lobby hosting, and room sessions.
* **Data Retention:** We do not sell, rent, or store personal user data on external studio databases. Identifiers and session tokens are processed in memory solely for the duration of the active multiplayer session.

## 3. User Data Deletion Requests (VRC.Privacy.4)
Users have the right to request the deletion of any stored personal data or identifiers associated with their account at any time, free of charge.

To submit a data deletion request:
* Send an email to **FishyStudiosbyPumkinfish@outlook.com** with the subject line **"Data Deletion Request"** and include your Meta/In-Game username.
* Requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. Upon confirmation, any associated platform mapping records will be permanently purged.
